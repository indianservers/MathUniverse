Integration
  Existing command execution, semantic results, quiz correctness, selection/movement,
  and speech playback events drive the presentation-only behavior engine.
  No neural model, command parser, conversation memory, or geometry calculation was replaced.
  Character position persists locally under math-robo-position; mouse and touch share pointer handling.
  Independent head, neck, torso, shoulders, elbows, wrists, legs, feet, pupils, facial paths,
  lights, and shadow are animated directly with requestAnimationFrame.
  Scheduler supports priorities, interruption, cancellation, bounded pending reactions, and recovery.
  25 facial expressions and 45 named actions are available.
  Idle look-around defaults to 25 seconds; resting defaults to 90 seconds.
  Both intervals and reduced motion can be configured on RoboCharacter.
  Speech animation is a playback-timed approximation, not phoneme lip synchronization.
  It starts on actual playback, freezes on pause, and stops on cancellation/end.
  Presentation listener/projection errors are contained and shown in diagnostics.

  Lab UI and strings are excluded from production bundles.
