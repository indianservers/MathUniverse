# Vector calculus, measurements and 3D accessibility

Central finite differences provide gradient, Hessian, Laplacian, directional derivative, Jacobian, divergence and curl with step size and estimated truncation error. Numerical midpoint box cubature, polyline length, triangulated area, Euclidean distance and screening centroid calculations expose method, domain, units, precision, tolerance, resolution, error, convergence, assumptions and warnings.

These are sampled numerical quantities. The UI never presents their agreement as a symbolic theorem proof. General line/surface integrals, Green/Stokes/divergence theorem studios, reliable Gaussian/mean curvature, geodesics, inertia and centre of mass are documented unsupported.

The canvas has synchronized nonvisual output: computed scene narration, exact definition, bounds, algorithm, topology warnings, measurements and a coordinate table. Narration is generated from computed nodes and explicitly labels approximation. Core controls are labelled and keyboard-operable; OrbitControls provide pointer/touch camera input. A complete certified screen-reader/keyboard camera study and optional sonification remain independent-review work.
