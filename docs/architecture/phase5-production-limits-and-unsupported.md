# Phase 5 production limits and unsupported mathematics

Implemented limits include finite parameter/Cartesian bounds, bounded resolution profiles, deterministic generation IDs, maximum implicit resolution of 36 in the main-thread extractor, bounded renderer DPR, extension operation/output ceilings, and explicit parsing rejection.

Known incomplete areas:

- crack-free octree/dual contouring, worker pool, stale-result arbitration UI and screen-space refinement;
- Boolean solid rendering and reliable volume/centroid/inertia for arbitrary regions;
- general surface/curve intersection, linked arbitrary slices and clipping planes;
- streamlines, particle advection, flux surfaces and vector-field slicing;
- symbolic multivariable CAS, theorem studios and differential-geometry certification;
- STL/OBJ/GLTF, PDF, SVG projection, animation/video and full accessible report export;
- full camera presets, keyboard camera commands, feature snapping and constraint-aware dragging;
- isolated third-party execution, remote collaboration and encrypted backup;
- official Wave 1 state sources, genuine review records and approved Telugu/Tamil translations.

Large or singular inputs fail through parser errors, empty-mesh/topology warnings, bounded profiles and declared partial convergence rather than fabricated output. Existing Vite chunk-size warnings remain a production optimization target.
