# State-board ingestion, curriculum diffing and localization

AP SCERT, Telangana SCERT and Tamil Nadu SCERT are registered independently as Wave 1 `SOURCE_REQUIRED`. Karnataka, Kerala and Maharashtra are schema/adapter ready only. No Phase 5 state board is certified.

The annual-update layer compares official-source checksums and section structures, identifies added/removed/changed and terminology/assessment changes, traces affected lessons and practice, creates review tasks, and expires affected certification. It never republishes automatically.

English, Telugu and Tamil plus Hindi-ready records preserve mathematical node IDs while versioning explanatory text. Terms support aliases, transliteration and pronunciation metadata. Translation states proceed through source approval, draft, math, language, teacher and accessibility review. Machine-generated content cannot reach `APPROVED` while flagged machine-generated.
