# Phase 3 accessibility, localization, copyright and known gaps

Learning screens use semantic headings, labelled native controls, visible design-system focus, live feedback, tables and textual alternatives. Lesson metadata requires keyboard, screen reader, high contrast, text scaling/reflow, reduced motion and nonvisual alternatives. Assessment design allows untimed accommodation. Phase 2 graph/geometry activities retain semantic/table alternatives.

Localization records locale, version, terminology aliases and independent reviewer state. English India is human-review-required; the initial Telugu terminology record is a machine draft. Automatic translation can never become approved mathematical content. Locale-aware number/search formatting and full translated UI are pending.

All authored explanations, examples and generated questions are original. Official documents are referenced by metadata, short structural facts and page citations; textbooks/model papers are not copied wholesale. Historical, cultural and Indian Knowledge Systems content requires reliable source and review before approval.

## Honest gaps

- The 919 existing lessons are classified as review-required under Phase 3; none was mass-promoted or deleted.
- Only one lesson has been migrated into the complete new schema, and it remains draft.
- Five practice families and one assessment blueprint are implemented; broad course coverage is pending.
- The ten studio kernels are tested, but dedicated polished studio pages and full symbolic methods are incomplete.
- AP BIE, Telangana BIE and full NCERT source ingestion are blocked by unavailable/unretrieved official documents.
- No genuine reviewers were available; approvals and course certification remain zero.
- Full lesson player pagination, assessment autosave/timer navigation, project editor, exportable report, translation UI and teacher review console remain future work.
- Automatic graph/construction scoring beyond declared invariants requires further Phase 2 integration.
