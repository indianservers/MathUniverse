# Public benchmark and product scorecard methodology

The versioned Phase 5 corpus contains 200 executable 3D cases: 20 explicit, 20 parametric, 20 implicit, 15 curves, 15 solid regions, 15 intersections, 15 slices/contours, 15 vector fields, 15 multivariable-calculus, 15 measurement, 15 singular/degenerate, 10 low-GPU and 10 accessibility workflows.

Each record declares input, domain, assumptions, behavior, exact/numerical/unsupported status, reference, tolerance, diagnostics, performance target, accessibility expectation and capability IDs. Unsupported solid/intersection/slice workflows pass only when explicitly classified unsupported; they are not counted as implemented mathematics.

The scorecard consumes evidence records and calculates correctness, unsupported honesty, critical accessibility findings, unverified claims and scaffold exposure. It cannot hard-code a leadership claim. Independent engines, mathematics reviewers, active teachers, accessibility auditors, security reviewers and low-end devices must reproduce results before any marketing claim.
